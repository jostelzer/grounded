## How do mRNA vaccines work?

**TL;DR** — The shot delivers a fatty droplet carrying genetic instructions. Your own cells read them, build one harmless viral protein, and the immune system learns to attack it. Two chemical tricks made it practical: modified letters that stop the body destroying the message, and the droplet, which both smuggles it into cells and acts as a built-in immune stimulant. Protection against severe disease is strong and lasting; against infection it is neither.

### The vaccine carries a message, not a pathogen

- It is a [lipid nanoparticle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipid_nanoparticle) (LNP) wrapping [messenger RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messenger_RNA) (mRNA); the particle enters cells, releases its cargo from the [endosome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endosome), and [ribosomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribosome) translate it into protein [Chaudhary et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-021-00283-5), [Pardi & Krammer 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-01042-y).
- Both licensed COVID-19 vaccines encode the same [antigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigen) — a prefusion-stabilised SARS-CoV-2 [spike protein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_spike_protein), a design ported from earlier coronavirus structural work — at 30 µg (BNT162b2) or 100 µg (mRNA-1273) [Polack et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2034577), [Baden et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2035389), [Corbett et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2622-0).
- The mRNA is transient and non-integrating, so a new vaccine means a new sequence, not a new process [Pardi & Krammer 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-01042-y); yield depends on the [untranslated regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Untranslated_region), codon usage and folding [Jin et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2024.108015).

### Chemically modified RNA letters were the unlock

- Modified nucleosides such as [pseudouridine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudouridine) abolish RNA's ability to trigger [Toll-like receptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll-like_receptor) 3, 7 and 8, so exposed [dendritic cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendritic_cell) make far less cytokine [Karikó et al. 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2005.06.008).
- Stripping double-stranded RNA contaminants by chromatography raised translation a further **10- to 1,000-fold** in primary cells [Karikó et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkr695).
- [N1-methylpseudouridine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N1-Methylpseudouridine) (m1Ψ), used in both licensed vaccines, beat pseudouridine by up to ~13-fold in mice [Andries et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jconrel.2015.08.051); it raises output by altering translation dynamics, not only by damping innate sensing [Rozman et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09945-5).
- The chemistry has a cost: m1Ψ causes +1 [ribosomal frameshifting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribosomal_frameshift), and immunity to the off-target products was detected in mice and humans — no adverse outcomes reported, and fixable by recoding [Mulroney et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06800-3).

### The lipid nanoparticle is a delivery vehicle *and* an adjuvant

- LNPs are ionizable-lipid carriers: charged at low pH to load RNA, near-neutral in blood, which limits toxicity [Cullis & Felgner 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-00977-6), [Hou et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41578-021-00358-0).
- In mice the formulation is itself an [adjuvant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunologic_adjuvant), outperforming an MF59-like comparator and driving [T follicular helper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T_follicular_helper_cell) (Tfh) and antibody responses through the ionizable lipid and [interleukin-6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interleukin_6) [Alameh et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2021.11.001).
- The parts divide the labour: the mRNA drives [type I interferon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interferon_type_I) that matures dendritic cells, while the LNP shapes where those cells sit in the draining lymph node [Castaño et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.11.023).

### T cells are primed by an unconventional route, and antibodies are refined for months

- In the first human trial, BNT162b1 elicited TH1-skewed CD4 and [CD8 T-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cytotoxic_T_cell) responses plus above-convalescent antibodies (phase 1/2) [Sahin et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2814-7).
- Priming skips the textbook route: in mice it needs neither cDC1 dendritic cells nor classical [cross-presentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-presentation), drawing on "cross-dressing" with ready-made peptide–[MHC class I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MHC_class_I) complexes [Jo et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10353-6), and gave about ten times more antigen-specific CD8 cells than protein plus an immunostimulant [Muro et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aec7827).
- Unlike an adjuvanted protein vaccine, mRNA generated [germinal centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germinal_center) B and Tfh cells in mice, with germinal-centre size tracking [neutralising antibody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutralizing_antibody) levels [Lederer et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2020.11.009).
- In people, lymph-node sampling (n=14) found germinal-centre B cells for at least **12 weeks**; across 1,540 clones [somatic hypermutation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somatic_hypermutation) rose **3.5-fold over six months**, leaving higher-affinity [memory B cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_B_cell), bone-marrow [plasma cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_cell) and Tfh cells lasting about six months [Turner et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03738-2), [Kim et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04527-1), [Mudd et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.12.026).

### Head to head, the RNA platform outperforms the alternatives

- A meta-regression of 35 randomised trials put symptomatic COVID-19 **70% lower** on mRNA than on [viral-vector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viral_vector_vaccine) vaccines ([incidence rate ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_ratio) 0.30, 95% CI 0.19–0.46) and 37% lower on [protein-subunit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subunit_vaccine) vaccines (0.63, 0.46–0.86); inactivated vaccines showed no advantage (0.98, 0.71–1.36) [Goryaynov & Gurova 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/vaccines12020130).
- Randomised mixing confirms it within-trial: an mRNA boost after a vector prime multiplied anti-spike IgG **nine- to tenfold** over a second vector dose (n=463; n=1,072); the reverse order missed non-inferiority [Liu et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736%2821%2901694-9), [Stuart et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736%2821%2902718-5).
- In a network meta-analysis of 63 studies, three mRNA doses ranked best against hospital admission (96%, 95% credible interval 82–99 pre-Omicron; 93%, 76–98 after) [Au & Cheung 2022](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2022-069989).
- A [self-amplifying RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-amplifying_RNA) vaccine also beat a vector vaccine head-to-head: relative efficacy 19.8% (4.0–33.0) over a year (n=2,366) [Ho et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41541-024-01017-5).

### Antibody level tracks protection, but does not account for all of it

- In the mRNA-1273 trial, [vaccine efficacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaccine_efficacy) rose with neutralising titre: 78% (95% CI 54–89) at a titre of 10, 91% (87–94) at 100, 96% (94–98) at 1,000 [Gilbert et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abm3425); it held for a third dose in the Omicron era ([hazard ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazard_ratio) 0.31, 0.10–0.96 per tenfold rise) [Zhang et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52348-9).
- The same gradient appears for a protein-subunit vaccine [Fong et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-35768-3) and for the RSV vaccine mRNA-1345 (hazard ratio 0.44, 0.30–0.65) [Ma et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61153-x); pseudovirus neutralisation is the strongest single marker [Benkeser et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.ade9078).
- Antibody is not the whole mechanism: in a vector-vaccine trial with 42 severe breakthroughs, efficacy against severe disease was **63.1%** (40.0–77.3) even below the assay's limit, and only **28.6%** (8.5–48.7) of total efficacy was mediated by titre [Carpp et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53727-y).
- T-cell receptor sequencing in 396 trial participants linked vaccine-elicited T-cell responses to lower risk of **severe** COVID-19 but not of symptomatic COVID-19 overall [Hertoghs et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105809); where prior infection is common, titre alone is a poor guide [Hwang et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2025.2473795).

### Protection wanes fastest against infection, and re-sequencing only partly fixes it

- Across 78 estimates, effectiveness fell **21.0 percentage points** (95% CI 13.9–29.8) against infection between 1 and 6 months, but only **10.0** (6.1–15.4) against severe disease [Feikin et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736%2822%2900152-0).
- One reason is anatomical: bronchoalveolar sampling found little neutralising antibody and no spike-specific B or T cells in vaccinated airways, unlike convalescents, despite strong blood responses [Tang et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciimmunol.add4853).
- Updating the sequence chases a moving target: a bivalent Omicron booster raised BA.1 titres 1.6-fold over the ancestral one (n=814) [Chalkias et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2208343), yet BA.5-bivalent sera barely neutralised the BQ.1.1 and XBB.1 variants that followed [Kurhade et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-022-02162-x), and pooling 12 studies (4,581 people) showed no gain over variant-matched monovalent boosters [Banga Ndzouboukou et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.virol.2025.110509).
- They still work where it matters: across 1.88 million matched pairs aged ≥65, an XBB.1.5 dose gave 57.9% (49.9–65.8) effectiveness against hospital admission and 75.2% (70.6–79.9) against death over 24 weeks [Andersson et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjmed-2024-001074).
- Repeated dosing leaves marks: responses stay imprinted toward the strain first seen ([original antigenic sin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Original_antigenic_sin)) [Song et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijid.2024.107198), [Baerends et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/ciad402), and boosters raise spike-specific [IgG4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunoglobulin_G), which correlated inversely with non-neutralising antibody functions [Selva et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.106087).

### Common side effects are mild; myocarditis is the one real rare harm

- Active surveillance of 11.8 million doses found no serious outcome above the pre-specified signal threshold, and anaphylaxis at **4.8** (3.2–6.9) and **5.1** (3.3–7.6) per million doses of BNT162b2 and mRNA-1273, by an unresolved mechanism [Klein et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.15072), [Risma et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaci.2021.04.002).
- The exception is [myocarditis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myocarditis) and pericarditis, confirmed as mRNA-specific across 99 million vaccinated people; the same study put Guillain-Barré syndrome and cerebral venous sinus thrombosis after the vector vaccine, not after mRNA [Faksova et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2024.01.100).
- It is rare and concentrated: 39.6 million adolescent doses gave **43.5** cases per million (30.8–61.6), 6.5 times higher in males, 3.6 times higher after the second dose, and lower than after infection at the same ages [Guo et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2023.05.049); US surveillance peaked at 105.9 per million second doses in males aged 16–17 [Oster et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.24110), and in England the excess after a second mRNA-1273 dose was 10 per million against 40 after a positive test [Patone et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-021-01630-0).
- Against that, in 45.7 million adults arterial and venous thrombosis were **lower** after every dose and brand, myocarditis rising only transiently (adjusted hazard ratio 3.14, 2.04–4.85 in the week after a second BNT162b2 dose) [Ip et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49634-x).

### The binding constraints are chemical and logistical, and the next generation targets both

- Manufacturing and cold chain, not immunology, are the practical limit [Rosa et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2021.03.038), [Pardi & Krammer 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-01042-y); the instability is intrinsic — backbone hydrolysis, nucleobase oxidation and freeze–thaw aggregation [Algaissi & Moni 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41541-026-01502-z).
- [Freeze-drying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeze-drying) works but is formulation-dependent: mRNA-LNPs survived lyophilisation in 20% sucrose, yet cargo retention and translation depended on the ionizable lipid [Lamoot et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1039/d2bm02031a), [Haensler et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1080/14760584.2025.2596674).
- Self-amplifying RNA carries its own replicase, so far less is needed: ARCT-154 reached 56.6% efficacy against any COVID-19 and 95.3% against severe disease at **5 µg**, one-sixth of the BNT162b2 dose [Hồ et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47905-1), [Blakney et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/vaccines9020097).
- Beyond COVID-19: against a licensed standard-dose influenza vaccine in 40,703 adults aged ≥50, mRNA-1010 cut confirmed influenza-like illness by a further 26.6% (16.7–35.4), at the cost of more [reactogenicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reactogenicity) (injection-site pain 65.8% vs 29.8%) [Leroux-Roels et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2516491); trials against other respiratory viruses are under way [Whitaker et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1097/qco.0000000000000948).
