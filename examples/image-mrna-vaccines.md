## How do mRNA vaccines work?

**TL;DR** — An mRNA vaccine gives cells a short-lived recipe for one pathogen protein, not the pathogen itself. A fat-based particle delivers it and helps alert immunity; brief production of that protein then trains antibodies, memory B cells and T cells. Performance still depends on the pathogen, variant, route and formulation.

### A temporary RNA recipe turns cells into [antigen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigen) producers

- The payload is [messenger RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messenger_RNA) (mRNA) inside a [lipid nanoparticle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipid_nanoparticle) (LNP): the particle protects RNA and releases it into the cytoplasm, where [ribosomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ribosome) translate it; the RNA neither enters the nucleus nor becomes DNA [Pardi & Krammer 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-01042-y), [Chaudhary et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-021-00283-5), [Hou et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41578-021-00358-0).
- Ionizable lipids bind RNA during particle formation but are closer to neutral in the body; endosomal escape remains inefficient and mechanistically disputed [Cullis & Felgner 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-00977-6), [Chatterjee et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2307800120).
- The encoded antigen is programmable rather than generic: COVID-19 vaccines used prefusion-stabilized spike, building on earlier coronavirus structural work, so a validated production platform can accept a redesigned sequence [Corbett et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2622-0), [Pardi & Krammer 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-01042-y).

### RNA chemistry quiets unwanted sensing, while the carrier supplies part of the alarm

- Unmodified RNA activates innate sensors; substituting modified nucleosides reduced signalling through [Toll-like receptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toll-like_receptor) 3, 7 and 8 and cytokine release from human [dendritic cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendritic_cell). Removing double-stranded RNA contaminants then eliminated detectable interferon/cytokine induction in tested cells and increased translation **10- to 1,000-fold** [Karikó et al. 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2005.06.008), [Karikó et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkr695).
- In mice, one LNP formulation acted as an adjuvant through its ionizable lipid and interleukin-6. Another mouse study linked the RNA-sensing protein [MDA5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IFIH1) and its type-I-interferon signal to expansion of killer T cells, while several proposed pathways were dispensable. These results may not generalize across LNPs or humans [Alameh et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2021.11.001), [Li et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-022-01163-9), [Verbeke et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2022.10.014), [Locci & Pardi 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10599-0).
- Chemical tuning has trade-offs: N1-methylpseudouridine reduced innate recognition but also enabled +1 ribosomal frameshifting and immune recognition of off-target products in mice and people; synonymous recoding reduced the signal, and no adverse clinical outcome was established [Mulroney et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06800-3).

### Lymph nodes convert a brief signal into durable immune memory

- Early human trials found neutralizing antibodies alongside antiviral type-1 helper and killer T-cell responses; killer T cells expanded after the first dose before neutralizing antibodies had fully matured [Sahin et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2814-7), [Oberhardt et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03841-4).
- Serial lymph-node sampling found spike-specific [germinal-centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germinal_center) B cells for at least **12 weeks** and T-follicular-helper cells for about **6 months**. Across 1,540 clones, somatic mutation rose **3.5-fold**, with affinity-matured [memory B cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_B_cell) and marrow plasma cells; cohorts were small [Turner et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03738-2), [Mudd et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.12.026), [Kim et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04527-1).
- Systemic vaccination built strong blood memory but weaker airway immunity: vaccinated participants had lower bronchoalveolar neutralization and no detectable spike-specific B or T cells there, unlike convalescents. This small study helps explain breakthrough infection but does not quantify its contribution [Tang et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciimmunol.add4853).

### Strong COVID-19 and [respiratory syncytial virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiratory_syncytial_virus) (RSV) results do not make every mRNA vaccine equally effective

- Before major immune-escape variants, pivotal placebo trials reported **95.0%** efficacy (95% credible interval 90.3–97.6; n=43,448) for BNT162b2 and **94.1%** (95% [confidence interval (CI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_interval) 89.3–96.8; n=30,420) for mRNA-1273 against symptomatic COVID-19 [Polack et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2034577), [Baden et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2035389).
- Neutralizing antibodies tracked protection: estimated mRNA-1273 efficacy rose from 78% (95% CI 54–89) at a neutralization titre of 10 to 96% (94–98) at 1,000; a 24-study model also linked variant neutralization loss to lower protection. Correlation does not show that antibodies mediate every component of protection [Gilbert et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abm3425), [Cromer et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/s2666-5247%2821%2900267-6).
- The platform protected older adults from RSV disease, and day-29 neutralization predicted lower risk; influenza trials instead found stronger A but weaker B responses than a licensed vaccine [Wilson et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2307079), [Ma et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61153-x), [Kandinov et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2025.2484088).

### Protection wanes fastest against infection, and variants can outrun the match

- Across 78 estimates, effectiveness fell **21.0 percentage points** (95% CI 13.9–29.8) against infection from month 1 to 6, versus **10.0 points** (6.1–15.4) against severe disease; only 3 of 18 included studies had low overall risk of bias, and all predated widespread Omicron [Feikin et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736%2822%2900152-0).
- Updated vaccines restored protection but did not end the pattern. A 28-study synthesis found bivalent boosters added **28–31% relative effectiveness** against infection and **58–62%** against severe outcomes. XBB.1.5 estimates fell from month 1 to 5: 52.9% to 26.7% against infection, 64.4% to 52.3% against hospitalization and 77.3% to 69.4% against death [Song et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2024.04.049), [Ma et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmii.2025.07.002).
- One target-trial emulation of 587,137 matched pairs—mostly older men—estimated lower 120-day effectiveness of 3.15% against infection, 25.25% against hospitalization and 30.25% against death. Residual confounding and population differences probably explain part of the gap between estimates [Ioannou et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.7326/annals-24-01015).
- Variant mismatch and immune history matter: BQ.1.1/XBB.1 substantially escaped BA.5-bivalent-dose neutralization, while XBB exposure often recalled B cells recognizing epitopes shared with the ancestral strain and recruited few truly XBB-specific clones in some people [Kurhade et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-022-02162-x), [Johnston et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2024.02.017).

### Myocarditis is rare, real, and concentrated by age, sex, dose and product

- Active surveillance of **11.8 million doses** found no prespecified serious-outcome signal and anaphylaxis at about **5 per million doses**; a separate matched national study found most examined events were not elevated but estimated 2.7 excess myocarditis cases per 100,000 vaccinated people [Klein et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2021.15072), [Barda et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2110475).
- A 99-million-person study reproduced myocarditis/pericarditis signals, although comparisons depend on background rates. In adolescents, pooled myopericarditis was **43.5 per million doses** (95% CI 30.8–61.6): 66.0 in males versus 10.1 in females, and 60.4 after dose 2 versus 16.6 after dose 1 [Faksova et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2024.01.100), [Guo et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2023.05.049).
- Nordic registers estimated **55.5 excess cases per million** after BNT162b2 dose 2 and **183.9 per million** after mRNA-1273 dose 2 in males aged 16–24. Booster risks were lower—8.6 and 19.5 per million young males, respectively—but remained elevated [Karlstad et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamacardio.2022.0583), [Hviid et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehae056).
- Infection is not a uniform comparator: across all ages it carried higher myocarditis risk, but among men younger than 40 the estimated excess after mRNA-1273 dose 2 was 97 per million versus 16 per million after a positive test [Patone et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1161/circulationaha.122.059970).

### Manufacturing speed still meets formulation-specific stability limits

- Large-scale production couples in-vitro transcription to DNA removal, purification, particle mixing, sterile filtration and product characterization; double-stranded RNA control, mixing reproducibility and scale-up remain bottlenecks [Rosa et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2021.03.038), [Hou et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41578-021-00358-0).
- [Freeze-drying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeze-drying) preserved one mRNA-LNP formulation for 12 weeks at room temperature and 24 weeks at 4 °C in preclinical tests. Continuous drying maintained transfection for at least 12 weeks at 4, 22 and 37 °C, but buffer, cryoprotectant and ionizable-lipid:mRNA ratio determined whether the result survived drying [Muramatsu et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymthe.2022.02.001), [Meulewaeter et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jconrel.2023.03.039), [Lamoot et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1039/d2bm02031a).
- Lipid chemistry also matters during liquid storage: piperidine-based ionizable lipids reduced reactive aldehydes, RNA–lipid adducts and functional loss at refrigeration temperature. These studies show possible routes to thermostability, not a universal room-temperature shelf life [Hashiba et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-024-06235-0).

### Self-amplifying RNA has clinical evidence; circular RNA remains preclinical

- [Self-amplifying RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-amplifying_RNA) carries replication machinery to copy the antigen template inside cells. A **5-μg** ARCT-154 regimen showed 56.6% efficacy (95% CI 48.7–63.3) against any and 95.3% (80.5–98.9) against severe Delta-era COVID-19; these results do not establish superiority across variants or over time [Hồ et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47905-1), [Pardi & Krammer 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-024-01042-y).
- As a fourth-dose booster (n=828), ARCT-154 produced non-inferior ancestral-strain and higher BA.4/5 neutralization than BNT162b2 at day 28; the trial measured immune responses, not disease prevention or durability [Oda et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1473-3099%2823%2900650-3).
- [Circular RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_RNA) may extend expression without a replicase: an optimized Zika circRNA vaccine produced durable single-dose protection without obvious dengue enhancement in mice. Human safety and efficacy remain untested [Liu et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53242-0).
