## What is known about the gut microbiome's role in Parkinson's disease?

**TL;DR** — People with Parkinson's disease have a reproducibly altered gut microbiome, but the average difference is small and varies strongly by cohort. Animal experiments show that microbes can modify Parkinson-like pathology; human studies do not yet establish whether microbiome changes cause Parkinson's, follow disease and constipation, or both. Microbiome-directed treatments reliably help constipation more than motor symptoms, and none is established as disease-modifying.

### Parkinson's has a reproducible group-level signal, not a single microbial signature

- A reanalysis of 10 case-control datasets (1,211 quality-filtered samples) found more *Akkermansia*, *Bifidobacterium* and *Lactobacillus* and less Lachnospiraceae and *Faecalibacterium* in Parkinson's; disease explained **<1%** of pooled community variation, versus 28–53% for study of origin. [Romano et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-021-00156-z)
- Independent syntheses broadly reproduce more *Akkermansia* and fewer butyrate-associated *Roseburia*, *Faecalibacterium* and Lachnospiraceae, while disagreeing on many other taxa; a 2024 meta-analysis pooled 14 studies (1,045 cases, 821 controls). [Nishiwaki et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.28119), [Bai et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.exger.2024.112444)
- A 10-study reanalysis (n=1,703) found that study and geography dominated variation and the adjusted Parkinson's effect was marginal; loss of microbial richness or evenness was absent in a separate seven-study analysis (n=1,067). [Toh et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.parkreldis.2021.11.017), [Plassais et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcab113)

| Evidence set | Scale | Main result | Limitation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Early discovery cohort | 72 cases + 72 controls | 77.6% lower Prevotellaceae; proposed four-family classifier | Single Finnish cohort [Scheperjans et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.26069) |
| US deep [metagenomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metagenomics) | 490 cases + 234 controls | >30% of tested species, genes and pathways differed | Cross-sectional, one region [Wallen et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-34667-x) |
| Four-cohort metagenomics | 244 newly sequenced; Germany and China added | Only one species was consistent across all cohorts; functional patterns were more stable | Heterogeneous populations and protocols [Boktor et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.29300) |
| Machine-learning meta-analysis | 4,489 samples | Within-study [area under the curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Receiver_operating_characteristic) (AUC) 71.9%; external AUC 61%; multi-study AUC 68% | No prospective clinical validation [Romano et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56829-3) |

### Human timing studies support bidirectionality, not proof that dysbiosis starts disease

- In 666 older adults at elevated risk, constipation, inactivity and possible [rapid-eye-movement sleep behavior disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_eye_movement_sleep_behavior_disorder) (RBD) tracked community composition, but olfactory loss, autonomic features, family history and aggregate prodromal probability did not. [Heinzel et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.26128)
- A cross-sectional series spanning 108 controls, 127 RBD relatives, 170 people with RBD and 36 with early Parkinson's found Parkinson-like changes in RBD after adjustment; a spouse-controlled cohort later found RBD-dependent microbial dynamics across established disease stages, not incident conversion. [Huang et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38248-4), [Lee et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-025-02095-w)
- After diagnosis, 74 patients retained depletion of several butyrate-producing taxa versus 74 household controls over 12 months, but microbiome-plus-diet prediction of faster progression improved AUC only from 0.58 to 0.64; a three-year untreated cohort retained just 25 people and its taxon–progression associations were explicitly exploratory. [Lubomski et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2022.875261), [Cilia et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3233/jpd-202297)
- Bidirectional [Mendelian randomization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendelian_randomization) reported 12 suggestive taxon associations: genetically predicted *Bifidobacterium* abundance corresponded to Parkinson's [odds ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odds_ratio) 0.77 (95% [confidence interval (CI)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_interval) 0.60–0.99), while Parkinson's liability also predicted microbial differences. This supports possible two-way effects, not a necessary organism or pathway. [Jiang et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1111/ene.15848)
- Truncal vagotomy was associated with lower Parkinson's incidence only after >20 years ([hazard ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazard_ratio) 0.53, 95% confidence interval 0.28–0.99); limited precision, surgical indication and residual confounding prevent causal interpretation. [Svensson et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.24448)

### Constipation, drugs, diet, geography and laboratory choices can imitate disease effects

- In 327 participants, Parkinson's status retained an independent signal, but region, age, sex, fruit and vegetable intake, catechol-*O*-methyltransferase inhibitors, anticholinergics and possibly levodopa also had distinct associations; the multinational meta-analysis likewise found that the inhibitor strongly increased Lactobacillaceae. [Hill‐Burns et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.26942), [Nishiwaki et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.28119)
- In a 300-person cohort, disease-associated shifts persisted after adjustment, yet stool consistency and constipation strongly tracked taxa and proteolytic metabolites. Transit can therefore be confounder, mediator or consequence; cross-sectional adjustment cannot identify which. [Cirstea et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.28052)
- Starting levodopa produced no corrected community-level change in 19 untreated patients over 90 days, but the study was small; an earlier “L-DOPA-naïve” metagenomic study included 31 cases, 90% of whom used other dopaminergic drugs. [Palacios et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2021.574529), [Bedarf et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13073-017-0428-y)
- Sixteen early studies reported >100 differential taxa while varying in collection, transport, extraction, sequenced region, bioinformatics and covariates. These differences plausibly explain part of the poor taxon-level replication. [Boertien et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.3233/jpd-191711), [Romano et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-021-00156-z)

### Barrier and metabolite findings are plausible, but depend on what and where researchers measure

- A small untreated study (9 patients, 10 controls) found higher whole-gut sucralose permeability, while small-intestinal sugar probes were null; a 2025 critical review found incompatible assays and concluded that consistent intestinal-barrier impairment is not established. [Forsyth et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0028032), [Derkinderen et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105796)
- Fecal [short-chain fatty acids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short-chain_fatty_acid) were lower in case-control studies (34+34 and 55+56 people) and related to stool inflammation, sex and taxa; plasma levels in 38 cases and 33 controls were not lower, and adjusted acetate was higher. Stool production, absorption and circulating exposure are not interchangeable. [Unger et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.parkreldis.2016.08.019), [Aho et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13024-021-00427-6), [Shin et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.28016)
- Multi-omics cohorts associate Parkinson's with altered fecal metabolites, sulfur metabolism and bile acids, but also show strong medication, constipation, body-mass and activity signatures. These findings nominate pathways; they do not show that a metabolite drives neurodegeneration. [Tan et al. 2021a](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.25982), [Hertel et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.10.035)

### Gut-to-brain mechanisms are strong in engineered mice and indirect in humans

- In mice overexpressing [alpha-synuclein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha-synuclein), microbes were required for motor deficits and neuroinflammation; antibiotics improved pathology, microbial metabolites worsened it, and Parkinson's-donor microbiota produced greater impairment than control-donor microbiota. Antibiotic depletion also protected a toxin-based mouse model. [Sampson et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.018), [Pu et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.102221)
- Injecting pathological alpha-synuclein into mouse gut produced staged vagal spread, neurodegeneration and symptoms prevented by truncal vagotomy; engineered intestinal epithelial expression likewise transferred seeding activity to vagus and hindbrain. Both test biological possibility, not spontaneous human disease. [Kim et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2019.05.035), [Chandra et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.172192)
- Human findings fit more than one route: phosphorylated alpha-synuclein appeared in colonic nerves in 4/17 people with isolated RBD, 1/19 with Parkinson's and 0/14 controls, while nonspecific staining was nearly ubiquitous; multimodal imaging supports distinct “body-first” and “brain-first” subtypes. [Sprenger et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.0000000000002126), [Horsager et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaa238)

### Randomized [fecal microbiota transplantation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fecal_microbiota_transplant) (FMT) trials disagree on motor benefit, while constipation improves more consistently

| Trial | Design | Motor outcome | Gastrointestinal or safety outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Cheng 2023 | Oral FMT, n=56 | Total score group×time −6.56 (95% CI −12.98 to −0.13), 12 weeks | Gastrointestinal scores improved; small single center [Cheng et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1080/19490976.2023.2284247) |
| Bruggeman 2024 | Nasojejunal FMT, n=46 | Off-medication motor change −5.8 vs −2.7 at 12 months | Temporary abdominal discomfort [Bruggeman et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eclinm.2024.102563) |
| Scheperjans 2024 | Colonoscopic FMT, n=48 randomized | Difference 0.97 points (95% CI −5.10 to 7.03) at 6 months | Gastrointestinal adverse events 53% vs 7% [Scheperjans et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2024.2305) |
| Wang 2025 | Fresh colonoscopic FMT, n=30 | Group×time −8.80 (95% CI −13.79 to −3.81), 12 weeks | 3.03 more complete bowel movements/week; no adverse events reported [Wang et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbi.2025.106086) |
| Figura 2026 | Colonoscopic FMT, n=59 | Difference 1.50 points (95% CI −4.28 to 7.28) at 12 months | Some time-specific nonmotor changes [Figura et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.78153) |
| Zhang 2026 | Repeated donor FMT, n=72 drug-naïve | Change −3.8 vs +0.1 at 35 weeks | Constipation −6.5 vs −0.7; mild self-limited events [Zhang et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41392-026-02604-9) |

- A 2026 synthesis of 11 studies (236 people; five randomized trials) found constipation improved consistently, whereas motor and nonmotor results were heterogeneous and most positive findings came from uncontrolled studies. Preparation, donor selection, dose, route and comparator also varied. [Chernova et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-026-01376-x)

### Probiotics help bowel symptoms; neither they nor diet have shown disease modification

- Three randomized probiotic/prebiotic trials improved bowel frequency over 4–8 weeks: between-group gains were 1.1 complete movements/week (n=120) and 1.3 spontaneous movements/week (n=72); another trial shortened transit by 36 hours but changed neither motor nor nonmotor scores. [Barichella et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.0000000000003127), [Tan et al. 2021b](https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.0000000000010998), [Ibrahim et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0244680)
- An eight-week Mediterranean-diet trial (n=36) increased fiber but did not outperform standard constipation care for symptoms or fecal zonulin; fecal calprotectin was lower at the threshold of statistical significance. [Rusch et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu16172946)
- No microbiome-targeted trial has yet demonstrated slower neurodegeneration. Symptom improvement, altered stool composition and correlations with colonic markers cannot by themselves establish disease modification. [Chernova et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-026-01376-x), [Scheperjans et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2024.2305), [Zhang et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41392-026-02604-9)

### Clinical use should remain symptom-directed while causality and portability are tested

- No stool profile is validated for diagnosis, prognosis or treatment selection: models lose substantial accuracy outside their training cohort, and longitudinal progression signals remain preliminary. Probiotics have useful evidence for constipation; FMT remains experimental for Parkinson's motor symptoms. [Romano et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56829-3), [Lubomski et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2022.875261), [Tan et al. 2021b](https://doi.org/10.1212/wnl.0000000000010998), [Chernova et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-026-01376-x)
- Decisive evidence requires incident prodromal cohorts with repeated diet, transit, medication and absolute microbial-load measurements; harmonized multicountry metagenomics and metabolomics; and preregistered, independently replicated trials whose primary outcome is motor progression rather than stool composition alone. [Boertien et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.3233/jpd-191711), [Huang et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-38248-4), [Derkinderen et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105796)
