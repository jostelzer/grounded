## Does creatine improve depressive symptoms?

**TL;DR** — Creatine is promising as a short-term add-on to established depression treatment, but it is not yet a proven antidepressant or stand-alone therapy. The pooled average benefit is smaller than a clinically important change, certainty is very low, and the best signals come from small, population-specific trials.

### The pooled effect is small and may not be clinically important

- A 2025 meta-analysis (11 placebo-controlled trials; n=1,093) estimated a [standardized mean difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardized_mean_difference) of −0.34 (95% [confidence interval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_interval) −0.68 to −0.00), equivalent to **2.2 points** on the [Hamilton Depression Rating Scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Rating_Scale_for_Depression)—below its 3-point minimal important difference. Results varied substantially, bias analyses favoured creatine, and [GRADE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GRADE_approach) certainty was very low. [Eckert et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0007114525105588)
- A newer review found only five psychiatric randomized trials (238 participants): four in major depression and one in bipolar depression. All tested **add-on 2–10 g/day for 4–8 weeks**; two trials had low risk of bias and three had some concerns. [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171)

### Positive signals are add-on and population-specific; null trials remain

| Population | Regimen | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 52 women with major depression | [Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selective_serotonin_reuptake_inhibitor) (SSRI) + 5 g/day, 8 weeks | Earlier improvement; review-estimated effect **1.13 standard deviations** at week 8. [Lyoo et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2012.12010009), [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171) |
| 100 adults with depression | [Cognitive behavioural therapy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_behavioral_therapy) + creatine, 8 weeks | [Patient Health Questionnaire-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patient_Health_Questionnaire) difference **−5.12 points**; exploratory pilot. [Sherpa et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroneuro.2024.10.004) |
| 18 antidepressant nonresponders | Antidepressant + 5 or 10 g/day, 4 weeks | **No overall advantage** over placebo. [Nemets & Levine 2013](https://doi.org/10.1097/yic.0b013e32835ff20f) |
| 34 adolescent females with SSRI-resistant depression | SSRI + 2, 4, or 10 g/day, 8 weeks | No symptom advantage versus placebo in the trial review. [Kondo et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00726-016-2194-3), [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171) |
| 35 adults with bipolar depression | Usual medication + 6 g/day, 6 weeks | Primary [Montgomery–Åsberg scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montgomery%E2%80%93%C3%85sberg_Depression_Rating_Scale) change was null (**0.231 standard deviations**); remission favoured creatine but was secondary. [Toniolo et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00702-017-1817-5) |

- Stand-alone evidence is limited to an uncontrolled 14-woman pilot in depression with methamphetamine dependence; 5 g/day for 8 weeks improved scores, but without a comparator causation is unknowable. [Hellem et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1080/15504263.2015.1100471)

### Brain-energy findings support plausibility, not clinical efficacy

- Creatine and [phosphocreatine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphocreatine) buffer and shuttle energy used to regenerate [adenosine triphosphate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adenosine_triphosphate), but spectroscopy findings in depression vary by brain region and mood state. [Kious et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.3390/biom9090406)
- In the adolescent dose study, frontal phosphocreatine changed by **+4.6%, +4.1%, and +9.1%** at 2, 4, and 10 g/day versus **−0.7%** with placebo and correlated with symptoms; a nested adult imaging study also changed metabolic/network markers. Neither association proves that the biomarker caused improvement. [Kondo et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00726-016-2194-3), [Yoon et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2015.11.027)
- In 22,692 US adults, higher dietary creatine correlated with lower depression odds (adjusted [odds ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odds_ratio) 0.68, 95% CI 0.52–0.88), but the cross-sectional diet recall cannot exclude confounding or reverse causation. [Bakian et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-020-0741-x)

### Short-term physical safety is reassuring; bipolar switching is the key warning

- Across 29 female studies (n=951), total adverse events ([risk ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_ratio) 1.24, 95% CI 0.51–2.98), gastrointestinal events (1.09, 0.53–2.24), weight, and renal/hepatic measures did not differ detectably; a 2025 kidney meta-analysis likewise found no filtration-rate reduction. These datasets were not depression-specific and were too small for rare harms. [de Guingand et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12061780), [Naeini et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12882-025-04558-6)
- Two of 17 creatine-treated participants in the bipolar trial switched to [hypomania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypomania)/mania; both bipolar participants in an earlier 10-person open study also switched. Causality is uncertain, but creatine should not be self-started for bipolar depression without clinical supervision. [Toniolo et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00702-017-1817-5), [Roitman et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1399-5618.2007.00532.x)
