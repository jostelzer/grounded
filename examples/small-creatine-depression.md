## Does creatine reduce depressive symptoms?

**TL;DR** — Creatine may accelerate and deepen improvement when added to an antidepressant or cognitive behavioural therapy, but the evidence is too small and biased to establish a clinically important benefit. It is unproven as a stand-alone treatment, and bipolar depression requires particular caution.

### The pooled benefit is small, uncertain, and below clinical importance

- The newest meta-analysis (11 placebo-controlled trials; n=1,093) found a [standardized mean difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardized_mean_difference) of **−0.34** (95% [confidence interval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_interval) −0.68 to −0.00), equivalent to **2.2 points** on the [Hamilton Depression Rating Scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_Rating_Scale_for_Depression)—below its 3-point minimal important difference. The I² measure of between-study inconsistency was high at 71%, trim-and-fill suggested small-study/publication bias favouring creatine, and [GRADE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GRADE_approach) certainty was very low [Eckert et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0007114525105588).
- The depression-specific psychiatric evidence comprises only five randomized trials (238 people; mean age 36; 26% male), all testing creatine **with another treatment** for 4–8 weeks; two trials had low risk of bias and three had some concerns [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171).

### Positive adult add-on trials coexist with null subgroup trials

| Population | Add-on regimen | Main result |
|---|---|---|
| 52 women with major depression | Escitalopram + 3–5 g/day, 8 weeks | Improvement separated by week 2; week-8 Hamilton-scale difference **4.4 points**, standardized effect 1.13 (0.75–1.52), remission 52.0% vs 25.9% [Lyoo et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2012.12010009). |
| 100 Indian adults, half male | Cognitive behavioural therapy + 5 g/day, 8 weeks | [Patient Health Questionnaire-9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patient_Health_Questionnaire) difference **−5.12** (−7.20 to −3.52), but 40% dropped out in each arm and the trial was unregistered [Sherpa et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroneuro.2024.10.004). |
| 18 antidepressant nonresponders, mostly women | Medication + 5 or 10 g/day, 4 weeks | **No dose or treatment advantage** over placebo [Nemets & Levine 2013](https://doi.org/10.1097/yic.0b013e32835ff20f). |
| 33 adolescent females whose depression had not improved with a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor | Same antidepressant + 2, 4, or 10 g/day, 8 weeks | Brain [phosphocreatine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phosphocreatine) rose dose-dependently, but depression scores did **not** differ from placebo [Kondo et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00726-016-2194-3), [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171). |
| 35 adults with bipolar depression | Usual medication + 6 g/day, 6 weeks | Primary symptom change was null (standardized effect 0.231); remission favoured creatine secondarily, while **2 of 17** switched to hypomania/mania [Toniolo et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00702-017-1817-5). |

- Evidence does not establish a sex-specific effect: early trials focused on females, the mixed-sex cognitive behavioural therapy pilot reported no sex moderation, and older non-depressed women had no mood benefit from creatine over 24 weeks. Depressed men, older adults, children, diverse ethnic groups, and long-term treatment remain under-studied [Sherpa et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroneuro.2024.10.004), [Alves et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0076301), [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171).

### Stand-alone efficacy is unknown

- The only stand-alone clinical study was an uncontrolled pilot in 14 women with depression and methamphetamine dependence; symptoms improved by week 2, but scores had already fallen before creatine started and there was no placebo group, so causation cannot be assigned [Hellem et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1080/15504263.2015.1100471).
- An earlier open add-on study also cannot establish efficacy, but it reinforces the bipolar warning: both bipolar participants switched to hypomania/mania [Roitman et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1399-5618.2007.00532.x).

### Short-term physical safety is reassuring, but rare and long-term harms remain uncertain

- Across 29 female studies (n=951), total adverse events (risk ratio 1.24, 95% CI 0.51–2.98), gastrointestinal events (1.09, 0.53–2.24), weight, and renal/hepatic measures did not differ detectably; surveillance methods varied and samples were too small for rare harms [de Guingand et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12061780).
- A kidney meta-analysis found a small creatinine rise but no reduction in filtration; most studies were short or moderate duration, leaving safety beyond one year sparse. The depression trials likewise cannot establish durability, relapse prevention, monotherapy efficacy, optimal dose, or safety in bipolar disorder [Naeini et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12882-025-04558-6), [Jeryous Fares et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/07067437251408171).
