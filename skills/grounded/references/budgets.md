# Review budgets

Generated by `scripts/sync_review_budgets.py`; edit `scripts/review_config.py`.
Source ranges are planning guidance, never inclusion quotas. Search saturation and relevance take precedence.
Popsci words are counted as the reader reads them: body prose without citations, standfirst, tables, captions or the scope note.
