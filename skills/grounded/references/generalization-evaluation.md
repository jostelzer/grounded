# Evaluate changes on unseen work

Read when changing scientific interpretation, synthesis or figure rules. The
durable target is preservation of evidence meaning across domains, study designs
and output styles. A discovered topic is a regression input, not a production
template. Prefer extending existing evidence and audit tools over a parallel
workflow or a new rule for each named example.

Before changing the rules, save the baseline and specify the behavior and
acceptance criteria being tested. Use independent case selection and judgments;
the implementer and judges must not receive held-out gold. Freeze candidate
rules before scoring. Once a case or judgment informs a revision, treat it as
development/regression material and use fresh cases for subsequent validation.

Compare baseline and candidate with the same source access, model, effort and
task budget where practical. Record departures. Include authentic primary
source context (tables, footnotes or supplements where relevant), supported and
unsupported assertions, and paired claims differing in a material dimension.
Sample unfamiliar domains and designs, including nonclinical and qualitative
work. Synthetic fixtures test deterministic invariants but do not demonstrate
real-world source interpretation.

Measure false acceptance, false rejection, coverage/abstention and material
qualification errors separately. Record raw denominators, severity and review
cost; do not hide failures in a composite score. The existing `score` command
reports false acceptance and rejection for supported-versus-other judgments,
alongside exact verdict agreement. Missing pairs remain coverage failures, not
correct rejections. Exact verdict disagreements should be adjudicated for their
practical significance rather than assumed equally consequential.

Also test complete tasks: a supplied-draft audit tests framing and compression;
an independently selected review request tests literature discovery, selection,
synthesis and final figures. A packet benchmark alone cannot certify those
capabilities. For figures, inspect rendered meaning as well as geometry; for
source access failures, reward justified narrowing or explicit uncertainty
without indiscriminate refusal of answerable questions.

Predeclare the comparison criteria and budget for each run. Accept an improvement
only with an observed reduction in the targeted material errors, without a
material increase in rejection of supported claims or new severe failures.
Report ceiling effects and small samples honestly; a tie or a perfect tiny
benchmark establishes neither improvement nor universal reliability. Independent
adjudication of disputed gold should be documented and applied to both conditions.
Preserve raw findings, prompts, rule versions and artifacts. Do not silently
rewrite failed runs into passing evidence.

Run focused executable regressions for changed invariants and the repository's
required checks. Static validation proves schema consistency, not judgment.
Keep the entrypoint short and reconcile overlapping references; avoid adding
universal image ratios, study quotas, mandatory rhetoric or phrase blacklists.
